How to read this. Every cell counts distinct creators — someone sampled five times in the window counts once, and someone who took three different products still counts once when several products are ticked. That is why this table can differ from the per-day charts above, which count creator-days by design. GMV slab and ethnicity are current creator attributes, not as-at the sample date: the slab is the creator's lifetime shop GMV band in Reacher today, so a creator who has grown since being sampled shows in their band today, not the one they were in when the sample went out. Ethnicity is inferred from avatar images, is partial, and is directional only — it is not self-reported.
